Make room for the cooking.

Cook Console keeps your recipes, next steps, and kitchen timers together, so you can spend less time navigating your phone and more time making a great meal.

YOUR OWN RECIPE COLLECTION
Create recipes with ingredients, directions, and step timers. Bring in plain-text recipes, mark your favorites, and find what you need with title search and tags.

SCALE TO YOUR TABLE
Adjust the number of servings and see ingredient quantities update instantly. Reset to the original recipe whenever you need to.

COOK ONE STEP AT A TIME
Follow clear, large instructions with simple back and next controls. A compact console keeps your current and upcoming steps close at hand. Return to the full recipe without losing your place.

KEEP EVERY TIMER IN VIEW
Run multiple named timers tied to recipe steps. Pause, resume, or add time as dinner comes together. Optional local notifications let you know when a timer finishes; on-screen alerts are available while the app is open.

YOUR RECIPES STAY YOURS
Cook Console works locally, without an account, ads, analytics, or cloud sync. Export a JSON backup of your recipes and cooking history, restore from a backup, or export cook history as CSV.

MADE FOR YOUR KITCHEN
A focused phone layout, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver support, and generous controls help keep cooking comfortable.

From a quick weeknight meal to a weekend batch cook, keep the next step within reach.
